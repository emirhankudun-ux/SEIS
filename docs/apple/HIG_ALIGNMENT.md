# HIG Alignment

SEIS follows Apple platform design principles without copying Apple apps or
creating fake system chrome.

## Alignment

- Clarity: surfaces should explain state and next action without visual noise.
- Hierarchy: sidebars, toolbars, module grids, and inspectors should guide
  attention.
- Feedback: status, health, demo mode, and safety boundaries should be visible.
- Consistency: modules should share status language, spacing, controls, and
  icon semantics.
- Accessibility: keyboard, VoiceOver, contrast, focus, and reduced motion are
  product requirements.

## SEIS Identity

The native product should feel cinematic and premium, but serious. Dark
surfaces, graphite structure, careful spacing, and small accent colors should
serve work, not decoration.

## Anti-Patterns

- copying Apple app layouts literally
- fake macOS windows in place of real product logic
- decorative glass everywhere
- unreadable labels or cramped controls
- motion that hides unclear structure
