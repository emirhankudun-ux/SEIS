# ADR 0003: SEIS Evolutionary Governance Throttle

Status: Accepted

## Decision

SEIS adopts the Long-Horizon Evolutionary Governance Throttle to prevent governance expansion from becoming noise.

## Levels

- D1 Uyarı: signal quality, ownership, or validation evidence starts drifting.
- D2 Dondurma: repeated drift stops expansion until recovery work is complete.
- D3 Yeniden Açılım: expansion resumes only after evidence, ownership, and validation recover.
